TELECOM CUSTOMER RETENTION POLICY

Policy 1: High-value high-risk customers
High-value customers with high churn risk receive priority retention treatment.

Allowed interventions:
- Premium recharge incentives
- Premium discounted data packs
- Personalized retention calls
- Loyalty offers

Policy 2: Medium-value high-risk customers
Use cost-controlled digital interventions.

Allowed interventions:
- Personalized SMS campaigns
- Discounted data packs
- Recharge incentives

Policy 3: Low-value high-risk customers
Retention spending should remain low.

Preferred interventions:
- Personalized SMS
- Minimal-cost engagement

Policy 4: Medium-risk customers
Avoid expensive interventions unless the customer has high business value.

Policy 5: Low-risk customers
No active retention intervention is normally required.

Important:
The policies describe business rules and eligibility.
They do not establish causal effectiveness of any intervention.
