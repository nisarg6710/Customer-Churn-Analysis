RETENTION DECISION PLAYBOOK

If recharge frequency is the strongest declining behavior:
Prioritize recharge incentives.

If data usage is the strongest declining behavior:
Prioritize data-pack interventions.

If outgoing usage is the strongest declining behavior:
Prioritize personalized engagement or retention calls.

For high-value high-risk customers:
Prefer stronger interventions.

For low-value high-risk customers:
Prefer low-cost interventions.

For low-risk customers:
Do not spend retention budget unnecessarily.

The system should always explain:
1. Why the customer is considered risky.
2. Why the customer is valuable.
3. Which behavior is deteriorating.
4. Why the selected action is appropriate.
5. Which policy supports the recommendation.

The system must not claim that an intervention will definitely prevent churn unless historical treatment/outcome data supports such a claim.
